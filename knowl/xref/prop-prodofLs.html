Proposition 7.3.2. Product of Lower Triangular Matrices is Lower Triangular.

If \(L_1\) and \(L_2\) are lower (resp. upper) \(n\times n\) matrices, then their product \(L_1L_2\) is lower (resp. upper) triangular.
