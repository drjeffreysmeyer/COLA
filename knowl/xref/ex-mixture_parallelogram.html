Insight 2.1.17. The Mixture Parallelogram.

If \(\vect{v}, \vect{w}\in \mathbb{R}^2\) and \(r,s\in\mathbb{R}\text{,}\) then the linear combination \(r\vect{v}+s\vect{w}\) may be visualized with its mixture parallelogram with sides determined by \(r\vect{v}\) and \(s\vect{w}\text{.}\)
