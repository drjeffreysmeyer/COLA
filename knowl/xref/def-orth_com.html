Definition 10.5.1. Orthogonal Complement of a Vector.

If \(\vect{v}\in\R^m\text{,}\) then the orthogonal complement of \(\vect{v}\) (or equivalently the line spanned by \(\vect{v}\)) is the collection of all vectors orthogonal to \(\vect{v}\) and is symbolized by
🔗
\begin{equation*} \vect{v}^\perp = \{ \vect{w}\in\R^m \ | \ \ip{\vect{v}}{\vect{w}} =0\}. \end{equation*}
