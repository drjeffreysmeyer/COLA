Proposition 2.2.7. Fundamental Algebraic Properties of Linear Combination.

If \(\vect{u}, \vect{v},\vect{w} \in \R^m\) and \(x,y\in\R\text{,}\) then
🔗
  1. (Associativity of addition) \((\vect{u}+\vect{v})+\vect{w}=\vect{u}+(\vect{v}+\vect{w})\text{;}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. (Zero vector) \(\vect{0}+\vect{v}=\vect{v}\text{,}\) where \(\vect{0}\) is the vector of all zero entries;
    🔗
    🔗
  3. (Inverse vector) \(\vect{-v}+\vect{v}=\vect{0}\text{,}\) where \(\vect{-v}\) is the vector whose entries are the negative of the entries of \(\vect{v}\text{;}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  4. (Commutativity of addition) \(\vect{v}+\vect{w}=\vect{w}+\vect{v}\text{;}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  5. (Scaling by 1) \(1\vect{v}=\vect{v}\text{;}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  6. (Associativity of scaling) \((x(y\vect{v}))=(xy)\vect{v}\text{;}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  7. (Distributivity of scalar sum) \((x+y)\vect{v}=x\vect{v}+y\vect{v}\text{;}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  8. (Distributivity of scaling across vector sum) \(x(\vect{v}+\vect{w})=x\vect{v}+x\vect{w}\text{.}\)
