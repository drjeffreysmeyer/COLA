Definition 3.1.5. Matrix Multiplication (Matrix with Vector).

If \(\vect{v}_1,\ldots, \vect{v}_n\in\R^m\text{,}\) \(A=[\vect{v}_1|\cdots | \vect{v}_n]\) and \(\vect{x}\in\R^n\text{,}\) then matrix multiplication of \(A\) and \(\vect{x}\) is the linear combination of the columns of \(A\) with corresponding scalars \(x_1, \ldots, x_n\text{.}\) In symbols, \(A\vect{x} = x_1\vect{v}_1+\cdots +x_n\vect{v}_n.\)
