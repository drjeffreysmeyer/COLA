Activity 4.2.9. Visualizing Intersections of Hyperplanes.

In this activity, you will reason about and visualize the intersections of hyperplanes in \(\mathbb{R}^2\) and \(\mathbb{R}^3\text{.}\) You will use these intersections to then reason about solution sets of matrix equations. The GeoGebra objects you should gain proficiency with are: Line, Plane, Intersect, and Intersect Two Surfaces. The four systems of hyperplanes you will be working with are below. Go through the parts for each one separately.
🔗
\begin{equation*} \text{System I: } \begin{cases} 2x + 3y = 13 \\ x + 4y = 14 \end{cases} \qquad \text{System II: } \begin{cases} x - 2y = 4 \\ x - y = 5 \\ 2y = 6 \end{cases} \end{equation*}
\begin{equation*} \text{System III: } \begin{cases} x + 2y + 3z = 2 \\ 4x + 5y + 6z = 5 \end{cases} \qquad \text{System IV: } \begin{cases} x + 3y + 4z = 11 \\ 2x + y + 5z = 9 \\ 6x + z = 7 \end{cases} \end{equation*}
