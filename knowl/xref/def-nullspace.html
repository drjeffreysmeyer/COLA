Definition 3.2.3. Null Space.

If \(A=[\vect{v}_1 \ | \ \cdots \ | \ \vect{v}_n]\) is an \(m\times n\) matrix, then the null space of \(A\) is the null space of its columns. In symbols,
🔗
\begin{equation*} \Null(A) = \Null(\vect{v}_1, \ldots, \vect{v}_n) = \{\vect{x}\in \mathbb{R}^n\ | \ A\vect{x}=\vect{0}\}. \end{equation*}
