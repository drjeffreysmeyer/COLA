Definition 2.1.12. Vector Addition.

If \(\vect{v}=\begin{bmatrix}v_1\\v_2\\ \vdots \\ v_m\end{bmatrix}, \vect{w}=\begin{bmatrix}w_1\\w_2\\ \vdots \\ w_m\end{bmatrix}\in \mathbb{R}^m\text{,}\) then \(\vect{v}+\vect{w}= \begin{bmatrix}v_1\\v_2\\ \vdots \\ v_m\end{bmatrix}+\begin{bmatrix}w_1\\w_2\\ \vdots \\ w_m\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}v_1+w_1\\v_2+w_2\\ \vdots \\ v_m+w_m\end{bmatrix}\) is their vector sum. The operation of forming their vector sum is vector addition.
