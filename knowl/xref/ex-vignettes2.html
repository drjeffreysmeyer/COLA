Quantitative Situation 1.1.4. Quantities as I travel.

I am always driving where I need to go. Today I will drive to campus, to lunch, to the grocery store, and then home. Even if I know directions, I often use my GPS in case there is something like traffic or a road closure. As I put in my destinations today, I realize that I am staring at list of quantities! In a given trip, each step of the travel instructions is the distance I drive, either in miles or feet, on a given road, until I turn onto the next street.
🔗
