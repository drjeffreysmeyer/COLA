Proposition 10.2.4. Algebraic Properties of the Inner Product.

The inner product satisfies each of the following.
🔗
  1. Positive definite. If \(\vect{v}\in \R^n\text{,}\) then \(\ip{\vect{v}}{\vect{v}}\ge 0\text{,}\) and equals 0 precisely when \(\vect{v}=\vect{0}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Symmetric. If \(\vect{v},\vect{w}\in \R^n\text{,}\) then \(\ip{\vect{v}}{\vect{w}}=\ip{\vect{w}}{\vect{v}}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Bilinear. The inner product is linear in the second input.
    🔗
    1. Additive. If \(\vect{u},\vect{v},\vect{w}\in \R^n\text{,}\) then \(\ip{\vect{u}}{\vect{v}+\vect{w}}=\ip{\vect{u}}{\vect{v}}+\ip{\vect{u}}{\vect{w}}\text{.}\)
      🔗
      🔗
    2. Scaling. If \(\vect{v},\vect{w}\in \R^n\text{,}\) \(k\in \R\text{,}\) then \(\ip{\vect{v}}{k\vect{w}}=k\ip{\vect{v}}{\vect{w}}\text{.}\)
      🔗
      🔗
    🔗
