Technology 3.3.15. The Transformation Visualizer Tool.

In this activity, you will use the Transformation Visualizer Tool to investigate and visualize how the entries of a \(2\times 2\) matrix affect the way the matrix transforms the unit square, and the plane more generally.
🔗
Instructions.

  1. Finding matrix entries to create a given transformation. In this part, you will discover how the entries of the matrix affect the associated transformation. To begin, play with the sliders, one at a time, and then mixing them and see what transformations you get. Now go through systematically: How can you get stretches (vertically or horizontally), shears (horizontally or vertically), reflections across either axis, projections onto either axis, or rotations (counterclockwise or clockwise)? Clearly record your observations.

  2. Creating visual and verbal representations from symbolic representations.
    🔗
    For each of the following matrices (I) use your Transformation Visualizer Tool to clearly depict how to the unit square \(S\) is transformed and (II) within a text box, use geometric words and complete sentences to verbally describe how the matrix transforms the plane. (Note: you may need to use multiple geometric words, in the proper order.)
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} A_1=\left[ \begin{array}{rr}1\amp -1 \\1\amp 1 \end{array}\right]\hspace{2pc} A_2=\left[ \begin{array}{rr}2\amp 0 \\0\amp -1 \end{array}\right]\hspace{2pc} A_3=\left[ \begin{array}{rr}2\amp 1 \\0\amp 2 \end{array}\right] \end{equation*}

  3. Relating the transformed unit square to mixture parallelograms.
    🔗
    If \(A=[\vect{v}|\vect{w}]\text{,}\) then how does the quadrilateral \(T(S)\) relate to the vectors \(\vect{v}\) and \(\vect{w}\text{?}\) Play with your sliders until you are confident in your answer. In complete and clear sentences, record this relationship.
