Proposition 2.4.18. Algebraic Properties of Span.

If \(S=\{\vect{v}_1,\ldots,\vect{v}_n\}\subset \R^m\text{,}\) and
🔗
  1. (Non-empty.) if \(\vect{0}\in \R^m\) denotes the zero vector, then \(\vect{0}\in \spn(S)\text{;}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. (Closed under addition.) if \(\vect{u},\vect{w}\in \spn(S)\text{,}\) then \(\vect{u}+\vect{w}\in \spn(S)\text{;}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. (Closed under scaling.) if \(\vect{w}\in \spn(S)\) and \(k\in \R\text{,}\) then \(k\vect{w}\in \spn(S)\text{.}\)
