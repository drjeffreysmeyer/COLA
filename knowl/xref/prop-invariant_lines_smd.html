Proposition 9.1.5. Invariant Lines and SMDs.

If \(\mathcal{B}= \{\vect{v}_1,\ldots, \vect{v}_n\}\subset \R^n\) is a basis, \(P=[\vect{v}_1|\cdots| \vect{v}_n]\text{,}\) \(D\) is a diagonal matrix with diagonal entries \(\lambda_1,\ldots, \lambda_n\text{,}\) and \(A=PDP^{-1}\text{,}\) then for each \(i\text{,}\) \(1\le i\le n\text{,}\) the line \(L_i = \{t \vect{v}_i \ | \ t\in \mathbb{R}\}\) is an \(A\)-invariant line with scaling factor \(\lambda_i\text{.}\)
