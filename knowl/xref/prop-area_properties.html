Proposition 8.1.3. Properties of Area.

Area has the following three properties:
  1. (Normalization.) The area of the unit square \(S\) is 1 (i.e. \(\Area(S)=1\)),
    🔗
  2. (Translation.) Translation does not affect area (i.e. \(\Area(\vect{v}+R)=\Area (R)\)), and
    🔗
  3. (Additivity.) The area of two disjoint regions is the sum of their area (i.e. if \(R_1\cap R_2=\varnothing\text{,}\) then \(\Area(R_1\cup R_2)=\Area (R_1)+\Area (R_2)\)).
