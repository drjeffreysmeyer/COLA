Definition 3.2.1. Column Space.

If \(A=[\vect{v}_1 \ | \ \cdots \ | \ \vect{v}_n]\) is an \(m\times n\) matrix, then the column space of \(A\) is the span of its columns. In symbols,
🔗
\begin{equation*} \col(A) = \spn(\vect{v}_1, \ldots, \vect{v}_n) = \{A\vect{x} \ | \ \vect{x} \in \R^n\}. \end{equation*}
