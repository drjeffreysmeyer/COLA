Proposition 5.2.3. Computational Quick Check for Containment in \(\spn(S)\).

If \(S=\{\vect{v}_1,\ldots, \vect{v}_n\}\subset \mathbb{R}^m\) and \(T=\{\vect{w}_1,\ldots, \vect{w}_r\}\subset \mathbb{R}^m\text{,}\) then \(\spn(T) \subset \spn(S)\) (i.e., \(\operatorname{Col}(B_T)\subset \operatorname{Col}(A_S)\)) if and only if \(\rref([A_S \mid B_T])\) has no zero-one rows.
