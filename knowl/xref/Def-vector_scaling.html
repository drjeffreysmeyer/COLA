Definition 2.1.7. Vector Scaling.

If \(k \in \R\) and \(\vect{v}=\begin{bmatrix}v_1\\v_2\\ \vdots \\ v_m\end{bmatrix}\in \R^m\text{,}\) then \(k\vect{v}=k \begin{bmatrix}v_1\\v_2\\ \vdots \\ v_m\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}kv_1\\kv_2\\ \vdots \\ kv_m\end{bmatrix}\) is the vector \(\vect{v}\) scaled by \(k\text{.}\)
