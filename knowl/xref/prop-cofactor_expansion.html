Theorem 8.3.7. Cofactor Expansion.

If \(A\) is an \(n\times n\) matrix, then
🔗
\begin{equation} \det(A)=a_{i1}(-1)^{i+1}\det(A_{i1})+a_{i2}(-1)^{i+2}\det(A_{i2})+\cdots +a_{in}(-1)^{i+n}\det(A_{in}).\tag{8.3.2} \end{equation}
We call this the cofactor expansion of \(\det(A)\) along the \(i^{th}\) row of \(A\).
