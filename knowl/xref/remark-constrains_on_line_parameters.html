Remark 2.3.12. Constraints on Parameters.

In quantitative situations, we may have constraints on the parameters. For example, if we are modeling a line segment, then we may have a constraint on the parameter \(t\) such that \(a \leq t \leq b\) for some real numbers \(a < b\text{.}\) In other situations, we may have a constraint on the parameter \(t\) such that \(t \geq 0\) or \(t \leq 0\text{.}\) In these cases, we are modeling a ray. In yet other situations, parameters may be constrained to a discrete set of values, such as \(t \in \Z\text{.}\) In this case, we are modeling a discrete set of points along the line.
